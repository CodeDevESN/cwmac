Institutional Credibility & Strategic Ambitions

CWMAC operates as a vital civil society organization bridging local community wildlife managers with national governance, policy frameworks, and international stakeholders.

We provide unified technical support, advocacy, and strategic resource coordination to empower rural communities to succcessfully protect, manage, and reap sustainable economic benefits from natural resources on village lands.

  • Enhancing good governance across community wildlife areas
  • Strengthening anti-poaching and natural asset protection
  • Forstering high-value tourism and private sector investments

Our Strategic Focus Areas

Driving institutional transformation and sustainable community-based natural resource management(CBNRM)

Policy & Advocacy

Giving rural and underrepresented community wildlife managers a unified voice in national wildlife policy and legislative developments.

Institutional Capacity

Training WMA leaders, building efficient administrative structures, and deploying transparent financial management solutions.

Tourism & Enterprise

Connecting Authorised Associations(AAs) with responsible investors to maximize community socio-economic diividends.
